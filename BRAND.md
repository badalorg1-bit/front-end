# Bayna (بينا) — Brand Reference (from official brand guide image)

Saved as the single source of truth for UI/UX identity. Supersedes the old "بدّال / BADAL" identity.

## Identity
- **Name AR:** بينا — **Name EN:** Bayna
- **Tagline AR:** أقرب مما تتخيل — **Tagline EN:** Closer Than You Think
- **Description:** منصة تربط بين الناس ليستأجروا أو يشتروا أو يشاركونوا ما يحتاجونه من ممتلكات وأدوات وخدمات — بطريقة سهلة، آمنة، وموثوقة.

## Brand colors (exact)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Blue 600 (primary) | `#2563EB` | primary interactive, logo gradient end |
| Blue 500 | `#3B82F6` | secondary / gradient mid |
| Blue 400 | `#60A5FA` | accents, light gradient |
| Blue 50 | `#E0F2FE` | soft containers / backgrounds |

## Typography
- **Font:** Tajawal (Google Fonts), weights 400/500/700/800 (fallback: similar molaric/rounded Arabic)
- Dark navy text is fine for body (existing ink `#0f172a`-family aligns with logo navy)

## Logo
- Two abstract people figures (heads = 2 circles, bodies = thick arcs) forming a circular "embrace", blue gradient `#3B82F6 → #2563EB`
- **Primary:** gradient mark + dark navy wordmark (بينا / Bayna) on light bg
- **Dark version:** white/blue mark on dark navy rounded square
- **Light version:** full-color mark, no box
- **Mono:** solid dark version
- **Banner:** white mark + white wordmark on blue gradient, right side tagline "أقرب مما تتخيل / Closer Than You Think"

## Values (icon row)
- آمن وموثوق / Safe & Trusted (shield)
- مجتمع حقيقي / Real Community (people)
- سهل وسريع / Easy & Fast (bolt)
- استخدام أفضل للموارد / Better Resource Use (leaf)

## Rules
- User-facing strings: "بينا" everywhere (was "بدّال"/"بادال")
- Interactive/primary = blue; escrow caution keeps amber/gold (semantic, not brand)
- Success/status greens may stay semantic (not brand colors)
- Internal identifiers (RPC names `*_badal_*`, storage path `badal-platform/`, localStorage keys) stay unchanged — DB compatibility
